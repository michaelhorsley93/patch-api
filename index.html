Questions

The things agents ask us first.

Where do the leads actually come from?

A team working the phones inside your community every day. Owners, tenants and past enquiries. Every lead is someone who has had a real conversation with a real person and said they are open to buying or selling. Nothing is scraped, bought from a list, or generated by a form fill.

Is the lead exclusive to me?

Yes. One agent per community, and while your subscription is active nobody else gets leads from your patch. If your community is already taken you go on the waitlist and we tell you where you sit.

How many leads exactly, and what if you fall short?

Between 10 and 20 a month, and we agree your number with you before you subscribe rather than promising every community the same figure. If we land under it, the shortfall carries into the next month on top of your normal volume. Fall two short and you get two extra, at no cost. If we still cannot deliver, we refund you pro rata. We do not keep money for leads that never arrived.

Why is it a range and not a fixed number?

Some communities are simply harder to work than others. Density, ownership mix, how much of the stock is tenanted and how often it turns over all change how many genuine conversations are there to be had. A villa community with 2,000 owners behaves nothing like a tower with 400 units. We tell you your number before you pay, and it does not move without us telling you.

Why not just hire someone myself for the same money?

You can, and plenty of agents try. At this budget you are hiring your first telesales person, paying their visa and seat, writing their script, listening to their calls and starting again when they leave. You are buying potential and managing it. Here you are buying output from people who already do this all day, with no employment risk attached.

I already post on social media. Does this clash with that?

No, it stacks. Social media builds an audience slowly and pays off much later, and a year of it might produce the volume you get here in a single month. This gives you named people in your community from week one. Plenty of agents run both, and plenty run this precisely because they have no interest in building a personal brand.

Can I cover more than one community?

Up to three. Two communities is 20 to 40 leads a month at AED 7,000, three is 30 to 60 at AED 10,000, so the rate per lead falls as you add. Most agents start with one, see what their conversion actually looks like, then add when the pipeline can take it.

Do you do off plan?

No. There are plenty of companies running ads for off plan launches. We do the harder thing, which is finding real buyers and sellers inside established communities.

How do the leads reach me?

Straight to your Telegram the moment each one comes in, with the name, number, property and a short note on what they said. Telegram because it is instant and reliable, and it takes one tap to connect after you subscribe. Everything also lands in your dashboard, so nothing is ever lost in a chat thread.

Are these leads ready to transact?

They are warm, not closed. They have said they are interested. Qualifying properly, building trust and winning the instruction is still your job. Anyone promising you signed listings is selling you something else.
